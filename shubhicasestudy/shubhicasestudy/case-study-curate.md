# Curate – AI-Powered Video Creation Module

**Role:** Senior Product Designer
**Company:** Genuin
**Scope:** End-to-end product design, UX, UI, and systems thinking
**Timeline:** ~1 month for this redesign, following an earlier version of Curate whose usability issues (surfaced through extended user feedback) directly shaped this iteration.

## Context & Challenge

At Genuin, our video-first community platform relied on a steady flow of engaging content. But creating high-quality, short-form videos was time-intensive and required editing expertise. This module also builds on an earlier version of Curate — extended feedback from that release surfaced usability issues in the creation flow, which directly shaped the priorities for this redesign.

**Key Challenges:**
- Repurposing videos from social platforms was tedious.
- Editing tasks like cropping and captions weren't accessible to all.
- Content output was inconsistent, limiting community engagement.

## Goals
1. Lower the barrier for content creation.
2. Enhance content quality automatically with AI.
3. Increase daily content volume and engagement across communities.

## Design Process

### Research & Insights

To better understand the problem, I ran mixed-method research alongside my cross-functional partners:

- **12 interviews** with community managers and content creators.
- **Analysis of content publishing behavior** across partner brands.
- **Stakeholder workshops** with Customer Success and Marketing.
- **Competitive analysis** of Opus Clip, CapCut, and VEED.
- **LogRocket session analysis**, used to track real user behavior and pinpoint exactly where people were dropping off or struggling in the existing creation flow — turning anecdotal complaints into specific, observable friction points.

**Key insights:**
- Most creators spent more time editing than actually publishing.
- The majority relied on 3–4 external tools before uploading content.
- Brands wanted faster publishing without sacrificing quality.
- New users found video editing intimidating, leading to lower adoption.

## Impact

- **60% reduction in video creation effort** (time-on-task testing).
- Contributed to more consistent daily content volume across communities.
- Community managers reported that Curate allowed them to publish content several times faster than their previous workflow.
- Enterprise customers appreciated having editing, captions, and publishing within a single interface instead of switching between multiple tools.
- Several brands highlighted AI captions as the feature that saved the most time during weekly content creation.

## What I Learned

Designing Curate reinforced that successful AI experiences aren't about automating everything — they're about removing repetitive work while keeping users in control. Close collaboration with Data Science helped balance automation with transparency, making AI feel like an assistant rather than a black box.

## Reflection & Next Steps
- **What worked:** Leveraging AI to automate repetitive tasks made video creation accessible to all.
- **Opportunities:**
  - Expand AI suggestions (thumbnails, music, hashtags).
  - Enable collaborative editing.
  - Integrate deeper analytics in the Brand Control Center.
